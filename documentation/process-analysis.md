# Process Analysis

## Analysis Approach

The analysis focused on identifying gaps between current operational practices and the processes required to support reliable inventory management, efficient warehouse operations, and accessible business information.

The main processes analyzed were:

- Receiving and inventory intake
- Inventory management
- Warehouse organization
- ERP utilization
- Reporting and business information

---

## Process Analysis

| Process | Current State | Key Gap | Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Receiving & Inventory Intake** | Newly received products were handled through manual processes before being fully registered and organized. | No standardized receiving workflow or dedicated receiving area. | Established dedicated receiving areas and defined a process for recording purchases and inventory information before permanent storage. |
| **Inventory Management** | Inventory information relied on system records, manual counts, and operational knowledge. | Limited access to reliable and up-to-date stock information. | Standardized product information, inventory records, and validation processes. |
| **Warehouse Organization** | Products were stored without a consistent location structure across warehouses. | Difficult product identification, organization, and traceability. | Defined standardized storage zones and reorganized products according to operational needs. |
| **ERP Utilization** | The existing ERP contained relevant operational information and functionality, but some capabilities were underutilized. | Limited use of available ERP functionality and restricted access to information. | Identified underutilized capabilities and implemented remote ERP access through MrCloud. |
| **Reporting & Data Accessibility** | Business information was primarily managed through the existing ERP, while monthly consolidations, cash closing, and purchase records were handled manually. Inventory information also required manual consolidation across warehouses. | Limited access to centralized and structured operational information, with several processes dependent on manual consolidation. | An Excel based consolidation approach was initially designed, followed by the implementation of remote ERP access through MrCloud. This improved access to operational information while Excel continued to support warehouse stock consolidation.|


---

## Key Process Improvements

The analysis identified several opportunities to improve process consistency and information flow:

### Receiving

A dedicated receiving workflow was established to separate newly received products from permanent inventory locations until purchase transactions and relevant inventory information were recorded.

### Inventory

Product and inventory information was standardized to improve consistency and support more reliable stock control.

### Warehouse Operations

Standardized storage zones were introduced to improve product organization, identification, and traceability.

### ERP & Reporting

ERP capabilities were reviewed to identify opportunities for better system utilization. Remote access through MrCloud improved access to the existing ERP database and operational information.

---

## Process Analysis Outcome

The analysis identified key operational gaps and translated them into practical improvement opportunities across inventory management, warehouse operations, ERP utilization, and reporting. These findings guided the implementation of standardized processes, improved inventory controls, and better access to business information.
