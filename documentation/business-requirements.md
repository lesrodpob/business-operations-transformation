# Business Requirements

## Business Context

Distribuidora El Palmar is a retail and distribution company that was experiencing operational challenges related to inventory management, warehouse organization, ERP utilization, and business reporting.

As the business continued to grow, inconsistent product information, manual processes, and limited access to structured business data made it increasingly difficult to maintain reliable inventory information and support operational decisions.

## Business Problem

The business required a more structured approach to inventory management and operational processes.

Key problems included:

- Inconsistent product naming and classification.
- Limited access to reliable inventory information.
- Manual inventory and reporting processes.
- Underutilization of existing ERP capabilities.
- Inventory losses and stock discrepancies.
- Limited visibility into sales and operational information.

## Business Objectives

The initiative aimed to:

- Improve inventory accuracy and organization.
- Standardize product and inventory information.
- Improve warehouse organization and inventory control.
- Increase access to operational and sales information.
- Improve ERP utilization and data accessibility.
- Support sales growth through improved operational visibility and reporting.
- Improve overall business performance through process improvement and data analysis.

## Stakeholders

Key stakeholders included:

- Business owners
- Operations and warehouse staff
- Sales staff
- ERP support provider
- Business & Data Analyst

## Current State

The business relied on a combination of ERP records, spreadsheets, manual counts, and other operational records.

Inventory information was not consistently structured, and several processes depended on manual verification and consolidation.

The existing ERP contained relevant business information and capabilities, but its use across daily operations was not fully optimized.

## Key Business Requirements

### Inventory Management

- Establish standardized product information and classification.
- Improve inventory visibility and stock control.
- Define structured warehouse storage zones.
- Improve inventory counting and reconciliation processes.
- Identify and monitor critical stock.

### Process Standardization

- Standardize key inventory-related processes.
- Establish dedicated receiving areas for newly received products.
- Define consistent procedures for recording purchases and inventory information.
- Improve the flow of products from receiving to permanent storage.

### ERP Optimization

- Increase the use of existing ERP capabilities.
- Improve access to ERP-generated operational information.
- Enable remote access to the existing ERP database.
- Identify underutilized ERP functionality and opportunities for improvement.

### Reporting & Data Accessibility

- Improve access to sales and operational information.
- Reduce reliance on manual data consolidation.
- Improve the availability of structured information for reporting and analysis.
- Establish a stronger foundation for future business analytics.

## Expected Outcomes

The initiative was expected to improve:

- Inventory organization and visibility.
- Product and inventory data consistency.
- Warehouse and receiving processes.
- ERP utilization and data accessibility.
- Reporting capabilities.
- Operational efficiency.
- Support for sales growth and business performance.
