
# Cash Closing Template

## Overview

This Excel template was developed as part of the Cash Management improvement initiative for Distribuidora El Palmar.

The template was designed to replace the previous manual cash ledger and provide a standardized structure for recording and consolidating daily cash activity across the three business cash registers.

The operational template is maintained in Spanish, as it was designed for direct use by the business staff.

---

## Purpose

The template is designed to:

- Standardize daily cash closing records.
- Consolidate information from three cash registers.
- Calculate total income and expenses.
- Calculate expected cash.
- Record the physical cash count at the end of the day.
- Calculate the difference between expected and actual cash.
- Identify cash shortages, surpluses, or balanced cash positions.
- Support monthly cash consolidation.

---

## Main Components

### Daily Cash Closing

The main form records cash activity for:

- Cash Register 1
- Cash Register 2
- Cash Register 3

The template separates income and expense categories and automatically calculates totals.

### Cash Count

The cash count section records the quantity of each cash denomination available at the end of the day.

The template automatically calculates:

- Total physical cash
- Fixed cash fund
- Total actual cash

### Cash Reconciliation

The template compares the expected cash balance with the actual physical cash and calculates the resulting difference.

The result is visually classified as:

- Cash shortage
- Cash surplus
- Balanced

---

## Automation

The workbook uses Excel formulas to automatically calculate totals and reconciliation results based on the information entered by the user.

The color reference distinguishes between:

- User-entered information
- Automatically calculated values

---

## Business Use

The template supports the standardized cash management process implemented across the three business cash registers.

It complements the ERP, which is used for cash opening, cash movements, and cash closing, while the Excel template provides a consolidated view of daily and monthly cash information.
