
# Cash Management

## Overview

As part of the Business Operations Transformation initiative, a cash management solution was implemented to standardize cash control across the three business cash registers.

The initiative addressed a manual cash management process in which daily cash activity was recorded in a physical ledger and monthly totals were manually consolidated.

The solution combined ERP-based operational controls with an Excel-based cash consolidation system.

---

## Business Problem

Before the improvement initiative:

- The three cash registers were not managed through a standardized cash control process.
- Sales were recorded through the cash register system, while other cash movements were not consistently recorded in the ERP.
- Credit payments, expenses, withdrawals, and other cash outflows were managed through manual records.
- Monthly cash totals required manual consolidation.
- Customer credit accounts and payments were managed through a manual ledger.

This limited the visibility, traceability, and accessibility of cash information.

---

## Solution Implemented

The cash management process was redesigned around two complementary components.

### ERP Operational Control

The ERP was established as the required operational system for:

- Cash opening
- Cash movements
- Cash closing
- Customer credit payments and account management

### Excel Cash Closing System

A standardized Excel-based cash closing system was developed to:

- Replace the existing manual cash ledger.
- Consolidate daily cash activity across the three cash registers.
- Provide monthly totals in a structured and accessible format.
- Simplify monthly cash reporting and review.

---

## Customer Credit Management

Customer credit payments were incorporated into the improved process through greater use of the ERP customer accounts module.

The module is used to register customer credit payments and manage outstanding account balances, reducing reliance on manual records.

---

## Operational Procedures

The implementation was supported by standardized procedures covering:

- Cash opening
- Cash closing
- Cash movements
- Customer credit payments

These procedures were documented to improve consistency and support ERP adoption across daily operations.

---

## Process Improvement

The new approach established a clear distinction between operational transaction recording and monthly consolidation.

ERP:

- Daily cash opening
- Cash movements
- Cash closing
- Customer credit payments

Excel:

- Consolidation of the three cash registers
- Monthly cash totals
- Structured cash reporting

---

## Business Impact

The implementation:

- Replaced the manual cash ledger with a standardized digital solution.
- Improved accessibility of monthly cash information.
- Standardized cash management procedures across the three cash registers.
- Increased the use of ERP functionality for cash operations.
- Improved traceability of customer credit payments.
- Reduced reliance on manual monthly consolidation.

Long-term quantitative impacts are still being evaluated as the new process continues to be adopted.
