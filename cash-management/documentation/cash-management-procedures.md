# Cash Management Procedures

## Process Overview

The cash management process was standardized to improve consistency in daily cash operations and reduce reliance on manual records.

The procedures cover:

- Cash opening
- Cash movements
- Cash closing
- Customer credit payments
- Monthly cash consolidation

---

## Cash Opening

Cash opening must be performed in the ERP at the beginning of each operating period.

### Procedure

1. Access the ERP through MrCloud.
2. Log in using the assigned user credentials.
3. Access the cash management module.
4. Select the corresponding cash register.
5. Perform the cash opening using the amount available at the beginning of the shift.
6. Verify that the opening has been correctly recorded in the system.

The cash opening establishes the starting balance for the operating period.

---

## Cash Movements

Cash movements must be recorded in the ERP during the operating period.

Cash movements may include:

- Cash expenses
- Payments made from the cash register
- Withdrawals
- Other cash inflows or outflows

### Procedure

1. Access the cash management module in the ERP.
2. Select the corresponding cash register.
3. Register the cash movement using the appropriate movement type.
4. Enter the amount and relevant information.
5. Confirm the transaction.
6. Verify that the movement has been correctly recorded.

Cash movements should be recorded when they occur to maintain an accurate record of cash activity.

---

## Cash Closing

Cash closing must be performed through the ERP at the end of the operating period.

### Procedure

1. Access the corresponding cash register in the ERP.
2. Review the cash activity recorded during the period.
3. Verify the physical cash available.
4. Compare the physical cash with the information recorded in the system.
5. Perform the cash closing in the ERP.
6. Verify that the closing was successfully recorded.

The cash closing establishes the final cash balance for the operating period.

---

## Customer Credit Payments

Customer credit payments are recorded through the ERP customer accounts module.

Customer credit applies to customers authorized to purchase on account, including internal and business-related accounts.

### Procedure

1. Access MrCloud through the desktop shortcut.
2. Log in using the assigned user credentials.
3. Access the customer accounts module.
4. Identify the customer account associated with the payment.
5. Register the amount being paid.
6. Verify that the payment is correctly associated with the customer's outstanding balance.
7. Confirm the transaction.
8. Verify the updated account balance.

Customer credit payments should be recorded through the ERP rather than maintained only through manual records.

---

## Monthly Cash Consolidation

The daily cash operations recorded in the ERP are complemented by an Excel-based consolidation system.

The Excel file consolidates information from the three business cash registers and provides a structured view of monthly cash activity.

### Procedure

1. Record daily cash activity according to the established ERP procedures.
2. Maintain the daily cash closing information for each cash register.
3. Enter the required information into the Excel cash closing file.
4. Consolidate the three cash registers within the monthly workbook.
5. Review the monthly totals.
6. Use the consolidated information for monthly reporting and review.

The Excel solution replaces the previous manual cash ledger and provides a more accessible way to review monthly cash information.

---

## Process Outcome

The implementation established a standardized workflow for cash opening, cash movements, cash closing, and customer credit payments through the ERP, supported by an Excel-based monthly consolidation system.

This replaced the previous reliance on manual cash records and provided a more structured and accessible approach to cash management.
