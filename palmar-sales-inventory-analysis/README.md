# Palmar — Sales & Inventory Analysis

Business & Data Analytics project focused on analyzing sales performance and inventory data from an ERP system.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes operational data from Distribuidora El Palmar, a Chilean distribution business.

The project combines SQL, Power BI, Power Query, and DAX to transform raw ERP data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

## Objectives

- Analyze sales performance over time.
- Identify high performing products, categories, and families.
- Analyze customer sales.
- Understand inventory distribution across warehouses.
- Identify products below minimum stock levels.
- Assess data quality and identify limitations in the ERP data.

## Data Quality Findings

The analysis identified several limitations in the historical ERP data, including incomplete product classification and inconsistencies in historical cost and pricing information.

These findings were considered when selecting the metrics included in the dashboard.

## Data Limitations

The ERP does not maintain a detailed historical record of daily physical stock movements between warehouses. Warehouse-level inventory is instead represented through current stock balances by product and warehouse.
